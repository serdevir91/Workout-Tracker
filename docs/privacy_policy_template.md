# Privacy Policy (Template)

This app stores workout data locally on your device.

## Data We Collect
- Workout logs and user profile fields entered by you.
- Purchase metadata from Google Play for subscription verification.
- Advertising identifiers (free version only, via AdMob).

## How Data Is Used
- Provide workout tracking and statistics features.
- Unlock premium access after subscription purchase.
- Show ads in the free version.

## Data Sharing
- No direct sale of personal data.
- Third-party services used:
  - Google Play Billing
  - Google AdMob (free version)

## Data Storage
- Primary storage is local SQLite database on device.
- Backup and restore are user-initiated via selected files.
